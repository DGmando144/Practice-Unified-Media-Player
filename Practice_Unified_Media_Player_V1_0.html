Milestone 1 scope

Working local audio/video loading, shared playback controls, seek, markers, looping, speed, fullscreen, and waveform for decodable audio. Audio waveform decoding is subject to browser support. Advanced independent pitch/cents/octave processing, video waveform, and system-wide Always on Top Float are not implemented in V1.0. Float remains excluded on iOS. Unsupported codecs will report an error rather than silently fail.

Button artwork and assignments can be imported from a Button Designer JSON export. Imported images are displayed directly; the library does not modify playback functions. Test MP3 and MP4 in Edge, then Safari on iPhone/iPad.
