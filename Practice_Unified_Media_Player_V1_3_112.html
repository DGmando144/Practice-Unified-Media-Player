Milestone 1 scope

Working local audio/video loading, shared playback controls, seek, markers, Trim Loop Mode, looping, speed, fullscreen, and waveform for decodable audio. Audio waveform decoding is subject to browser support. iOS audio uses the SoundTouchJS decoded-buffer AudioWorklet (requires HTTPS and network access); desktop audio retains its original processing. Video is unchanged. Video waveform extraction is attempted when the browser can decode the video audio track; unavailable codecs show a fallback. System-wide Always on Top Float is not implemented. Pitch shifting may produce artifacts, particularly on complex music. The legacy fallback can crackle under CPU load. Float remains excluded on iOS. Unsupported codecs will report an error rather than silently fail.

Playback button order follows your corrected Button Designer V1.9 JSON; the reference screenshot is used only for Speed/Pitch layout. Button artwork and assignments can be imported from a Button Designer JSON export. Imported images are displayed directly; the library does not modify playback functions.
